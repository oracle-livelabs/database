# Top 10 Must-Have Enterprise Agents: Design, Build, and Productize Them Today

## Introduction

Welcome to bootcamp session **BOO6096**. Discover ten practical enterprise AI agent patterns across procurement, finance, HR, sales, customer service, IT, field operations, compliance, knowledge management, and executive insights.

The presentation and demos introduce a repeatable approach to designing enterprise agents: business process mapping, data and system integration, governance, human-in-the-loop workflows, observability, lifecycle management, and reuse. Learn how to prioritize use cases and scale governed enterprise AI across your organization.

**Watch the presentation and demos for the different agents.** If you want to follow along and try hands-on with the presenters, use the credentials provided to sign in to **Oracle AI Database Private Agent Factory** and try the demonstrated agents yourself.

**Estimated time:** The scheduled bootcamp session; hands-on participation is optional.

### Objectives

* Recognize practical agent patterns across ten core business functions
* Understand the design considerations for developing and productizing enterprise agents
* Follow the presenters' demos and, optionally, build and test agents in Private Agent Factory

### Prerequisites

* Attend the bootcamp presentation and demos
* For optional hands-on participation, have a browser and the Private Agent Factory URL and credentials provided by the presenters

## Task 1: Watch the presentation and agent demos

1. Watch the presenters introduce the different enterprise agents and demonstrate how they work.

2. For each agent, consider the business process, required data and integrations, governance, human review, and how the solution can be observed, maintained, and reused.

3. Use the presenters' prioritization framework to identify the agent patterns most relevant to your organization.

## Task 2: Follow along with the presenters (optional)

1. Open the **Private Agent Factory URL provided by the presenters** in your browser.

2. **Sign in using the credentials provided during the session.** If you need access details or help signing in, ask a presenter.

3. Follow the presenters as they design, build, and test the agents. Use the data, prompts, and templates provided for the demo to try the steps yourself in Private Agent Factory.

## Summary

Use the demonstrated agent patterns and repeatable design approach to prioritize opportunities for your organization. Continue experimenting with the presenters during the optional hands-on portion of the session.
