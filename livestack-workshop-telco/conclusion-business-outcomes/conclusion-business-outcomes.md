3. Use this explanation when you describe the workshop to someone else.

    Seer Comms can move from network pressure to service-order, signal, impact, response-location, and predictive-risk evidence without handing facts from one specialist system to another. Oracle AI Database 26ai keeps the data models and model scores connected in one governed foundation. Autonomous Database adds managed patching, backups, scaling, and security maintenance, so the team can focus on explaining and improving the network experience. The result is fewer sensitive copies, fewer reconciliation points, and faster investigation when a decision needs evidence.
