# Welcome to AppGen: Meet Oracle's AI App Development Platform

## Introduction

Welcome to meetup session **MET6099**. This is a walk-up meetup: join at any point, start the environment, follow the in-app instructions, and choose one or more short activities.

**Estimated Time:** 15–20 minutes total, including setup and feedback. One activity is enough; you do not need to stay for the full scheduled session.

Your feedback is the goal of this workshop. Notice what is clear, what is confusing, and where you need help as you try AppGen.

### Objectives

* Get started in AppGen using the on-screen instructions.
* Try a realistic sample app or turn an idea into an app preview.
* See how an app can be refined and inspect generated code.
* Provide structured feedback about clarity, ease, results, and friction.

### Prerequisites

* A browser and willingness to try the AppGen environment; follow the on-screen quick-start guidance.
* An app idea is optional. No coding experience is needed; OCI connectors and database connections are optional for the activities.

## Quick Start: Start the environment and follow the instructions

**Time:** 2–3 minutes.

1. Open the **AppGen URL provided at the meetup** in your browser. Ask a facilitator if you need access details.

2. Start or spin up the environment as directed, and use the in-app instructions to orient yourself.

3. Choose one, two, or all three activities below. Keep each try short and reserve 3–4 minutes at the end for feedback so your total visit stays within 15–20 minutes.

## Task 1: Explore a pre-built app

**Time:** About 3–4 minutes. This activity is optional.

Choose any one of the three sample apps and complete a small task:

* **People Connection:** Find someone by workplace or role, open a profile, and follow a manager or cross-team connection.
* **Invoice IQ:** Review the sample invoice against its purchase order, receipt, and policy; decide whether the explanation supports the recommendation.
* **Database Performance Dashboard:** Find a performance spike, open the related SQL details, and identify what the statistics or plan suggest.

Notice what helped you find the next step, and anything unclear or unexpected.

## Task 2: Generate an app from an idea

**Time:** About 3–4 minutes. This activity is optional.

1. Use **Build Your Own** and describe a small app idea in plain language. Include who it is for, what they need to do, and the information they need. Use sample data; connectors or existing database context are optional.

    **Prompt starter:** Create a [type] app for [user] to [goal]. Show [key information] and let them [action].

2. Review the first generated preview and decide whether it reflects your main goal: **yes**, **partly**, or **no**. Keep that answer for your feedback.

## Task 3: Refine an app and inspect the result

**Time:** About 3–4 minutes. This activity is optional.

1. In an app you opened or generated, ask the **AppGen Agent** for one specific change in plain language, then check the preview.

2. If time allows, open **Code** and locate the generated React and Node.js files.

3. Notice whether the change was easy to request and verify.

## Feedback

**Time:** About 3–4 minutes.

Use the feedback method provided at the meetup. Ask a facilitator where to submit your responses if needed. Share what happened even if you could not finish an activity.

1. **Activity tried:** Which activity or activities did you try? For a sample app, include its name.

2. **Completion:** For each activity, were you able to complete it **on your own**, **with a hint**, or **not yet**?

3. **Ease:** How easy was each activity on a scale of **1–5**, where **1 = very difficult** and **5 = very easy**?

4. **Clarity:** What was clear or confusing?

5. **First preview:** If you generated an app, did the first preview fit your goal: **yes**, **partly**, or **no**? Skip this question if you did not generate an app.

6. **Friction or gaps:** What was the biggest friction point or missing capability?

7. **Future use:** Would you use AppGen for similar work: **yes**, **no**, or **depends**? If it depends, what would need to change?

One activity and feedback are enough. Wrap up within 15–20 minutes total.
