# Workshop Help

## Introduction

Use these checks when a step fails. Keep logs and error messages, and remove private information before sharing them.

Estimated Time: 5-15 minutes for initial checks.

### Objectives

- Find the correct troubleshooting or recovery path.
- Collect useful evidence for the workshop owner or tenancy administrator.

## Task 1: Choose the next check

1. For OCI sign-in, permissions, service limits, or compartment selection, review [Lab 1 prerequisites](../environment/environment.md) with your tenancy administrator.

1. For OSMH policies, dynamic groups, and source access, review [Administrator setup](../administrator-setup/administrator-setup.md). Resolve required access before continuing.

1. For SSH, migration, boot, rebuild, or backup restore problems, follow [Recovery](../recovery/recovery.md). Do not discard logs or terminate resources containing needed results.

1. If the image link is unavailable, contact the workshop owner for the prepared RHEL 9.8 image URL. Do not share token-bearing URLs in public issues or screenshots.

1. If you need workshop-content help, provide the lab and task, the command or Console action, expected and actual results, and sanitized error text. Include your region and software versions. Keep private keys, credentials, and image tokens out of the report.

1. Use the [LiveLabs help guide](https://livelabs.oracle.com/cdn/common/labs/need-help/need-help-freetier.md) for general workshop support. Retain the authors listed below when publishing this conversion.
