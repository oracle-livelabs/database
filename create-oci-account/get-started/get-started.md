# Get Started

## Introduction

Use this short lab to confirm that you are ready to begin the OCI Free Tier sign-up process. The next three labs guide you through preparation, account creation, and first sign-in.

Estimated Time: 2 minutes

### Objectives

In this lab, you will:

- Confirm that you have the required browser and email access.
- Review the sequence of the three hands-on labs.
- Open the official OCI Free Tier registration page.

## Task 1: Confirm that you are ready

1. Confirm that you can access a supported browser and the email inbox you will use for the Oracle account.

2. Confirm that you have authority to create the account and, if requested, access to a valid payment verification method.

3. Open the OCI Free Tier registration page. Leave it open for the next lab.

    ```text
    https://signup.oraclecloud.com/
    ```

## Task 2: Follow the lab sequence

1. Complete **Lab 1: Prepare to Sign Up** to gather the required information and choose a home region.

2. Complete **Lab 2: Create and Activate Your Account** to submit the account request.

3. Complete **Lab 3: Sign In to OCI for the First Time** after Oracle sends the activation email.
