# Sign In to OCI for the First Time

## Introduction

After Oracle provisions the account, the activation or welcome email contains the details needed for first access. Use that email rather than a bookmarked URL so you enter the correct account context.

Estimated Time: 8 minutes

### Objectives

In this lab, you will:

- Find the account activation or welcome email.
- Sign in to the OCI Console for the first time.
- Confirm the account details needed for future access.

## Task 1: Find the activation email

1. Search the registration inbox and its spam or junk folder for an Oracle activation or welcome email. Useful search terms include the cloud account name, **Oracle**, **Cloud Console**, **activate your profile**, and **activate your account**.

2. Open the message only if it is an expected Oracle account message. Confirm that it corresponds to the account you just created.

3. If the activation email has not arrived after a reasonable wait, do not create a duplicate account. Contact Oracle support and ask for the activation email to be resent.

## Task 2: Sign in to the OCI Console

1. Select the activation link or the official access link in the email. Follow the prompted activation or password-change steps.

    ![Oracle documentation screenshot of the OCI account sign-in screen](https://docs.oracle.com/en/learn/get-started-with-oci-and-oci-console/images/Getting-Started-OCI22.png)

    > Oracle documentation screenshot. Use the account details and sign-in path in your activation email; the live OCI sign-in flow can vary.

2. Sign in with the cloud account name or tenancy information, user name, and password indicated by the current sign-in flow. Do not save credentials in a shared browser profile.

3. Confirm that the OCI Console opens. You should be able to see the Console navigation and the selected home region. This completes the workshop; do not create resources unless a later workshop instructs you to do so.

## Task 3: Record safe recovery information

1. Record the cloud account name, home region, and official sign-in URL in an approved password manager or organization-approved account inventory.

2. Keep the activation email until you have confirmed access and your organization has recorded the account owner and recovery process.

3. Review the Free Tier page before provisioning a resource so you understand the current promotion and Always Free terms.
