# Prepare to Sign Up

## Introduction

Before opening the registration form, gather the information you will use and make two important decisions: which email inbox will receive the account messages and which home region best fits your expected work. A home region cannot be changed after sign-up.

Estimated Time: 5 minutes

### Objectives

In this lab, you will:

- Identify the information requested during Free Tier registration.
- Select an appropriate email inbox and home region.
- Open the official Free Tier sign-up page.

## Task 1: Gather the required information

1. Confirm that you can access the email inbox you will use. Oracle sends the email verification and later activation information to this address.

2. Have the following ready. The form can request different details depending on your country.

    - Your legal name, country or territory, address, and phone number.
    - A mobile phone number and a payment verification method, if requested.
    - A new, strong password that meets the policy displayed on the form.

3. Keep payment data, passwords, verification links, and one-time codes private. Do not put them in a document, chat, or screenshot for this workshop.

## Task 2: Choose a home region deliberately

1. Review the OCI regions available for your country and the services you plan to use.

    - Your home region is the geographic location where the account and identity resources are created.
    - You cannot change the home region after registration.
    - Always Free Autonomous AI Database and compute instances can be provisioned only in the home region.

2. Choose a region near your users or required data location, subject to your organization's policies. If you are unsure, pause and confirm the choice with the account owner before continuing.

## Task 3: Open the official registration page

1. Open the Oracle Cloud Free Tier registration page in a new browser tab.

    ```text
    https://signup.oraclecloud.com/
    ```

2. Keep the tab open for the next lab. Do not start a second registration with a different email address if you suspect an account already exists; first search the inbox and contact Oracle support if needed.
