# Create and Activate Your Account

## Introduction

Use the official registration form to create the account. The form verifies the email address, collects the account and contact information, and may ask for payment verification. A temporary verification hold can appear on the payment method; Oracle states that the card is not charged unless you choose to upgrade the cloud account.

Estimated Time: 12 minutes

### Objectives

In this lab, you will:

- Complete the account information and email verification steps.
- Set a cloud account name and choose the home region.
- Submit the Free Tier request and identify the activation confirmation.

## Task 1: Complete account and email verification

1. On the registration page, enter your country or territory, legal name, and email address. Complete the human-verification challenge when it appears.

    ![Oracle documentation screenshot showing the Sign Up entry point on the Oracle Cloud page](https://docs.oracle.com/en/learn/get-started-with-oci-and-oci-console/images/Getting-Started-OCI3.png)

    > Oracle documentation screenshot. The live registration form, labels, and sequence can vary by country and over time.

2. Select **Verify my email**. Open the Oracle email in the same inbox you chose in the prior lab and follow its verification link before it expires.

3. Return to the registration page and create a password that meets the policy shown there. Keep the password in an approved password manager; do not paste it into this workshop or share it.

## Task 2: Set account details and verify payment information

1. Enter the requested account and contact details. Choose a cloud account name that you can recognize later; it is used as part of your account identification and sign-in flow.

2. Select the home region you decided on in the previous lab. Verify it carefully before continuing because it cannot be changed after sign-up.

3. If the form asks for a payment verification method, use a valid method that belongs to you or that you are authorized to use. Complete the verification only on Oracle's secure registration page.

    > The payment provider might display a small temporary verification hold. Oracle documents that the method is not charged unless you explicitly upgrade the account.

## Task 3: Submit the Free Tier request

1. Read the terms presented on the registration page. If they are acceptable, accept them and select **Start my free trial** or the equivalent submit action shown by the current form.

2. Wait for Oracle to process the request. Provisioning can take a few minutes.

3. Check the email inbox, including spam or junk folders, for the activation or welcome email. Save the cloud account name and the official sign-in URL in your approved password manager or account record.
