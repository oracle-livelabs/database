# Create Your OCI Free Tier Account

## Introduction

This workshop shows you how to create an Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) Free Tier account and confirm that you can access the OCI Console. It does not provision cloud resources.

OCI Free Tier includes a time-limited promotional trial and Always Free offers. Read the current terms and available services before you begin, because availability and eligibility can vary by country.

### Prerequisites

- A supported browser and a personal or organizational email inbox.
- A mobile phone number and a payment verification method if Oracle requests them.
- Authorization to create the account. Do not use another person's identity or payment details.

### Objectives

- Prepare the information required for sign-up.
- Create and verify an OCI Free Tier account.
- Choose a home region with an understanding of its impact.
- Sign in to the OCI Console after activation.

Estimated Workshop Time: 25 minutes
